Get started

A few quick steps, done once. You will need Chrome and your ESPN fantasy basketball league.

  1. 1

    Add the FBL Advisor bookmark

    Do this now

    Drag THIS link to the Chrome bookmarks bar (press Ctrl+Shift+B if the bar is hidden). Do not bookmark this panel page with the browser star; that saves this panel instead of the advisor.

    FBL Advisor — drag THIS link to bookmarks

    __BOOKMARKLET_NOTE__

    If dragging is inconvenient, copy the URL below, create a Chrome bookmark, and paste it into the bookmark’s URL field.

    1. Right-click the Chrome bookmarks bar and choose Add page.
    2. Name it FBL Advisor.
    3. Paste the copied text into URL, then save.
  2. 2

    Link your ESPN league

    Next

    On ESPN, open My Team for your fantasy basketball league and copy the full address from the browser’s address bar.

    Paste your team page address, then press Save.

  3. 3

    Choose your team

    Next

    Open a practice draft (or your real draft) room for this league on ESPN, then click the FBL Advisor bookmark. You can pick your team here or inside the ESPN overlay.

    Waiting for the ESPN team list…


  4. 4

    Capture the player catalog in a practice draft

    Next

    In a practice draft room on ESPN, click the FBL Advisor bookmark and press Capture inputs. Keep that browser tab visible (do not switch tabs or minimize the window) until it says it has finished.

  5. 5

    Draft day

    Later

    Open your real draft room on ESPN and click the FBL Advisor bookmark. Confirm it says Loaded saved scan, then press Start observing.
